Reference definitions already used by this project (not current freight records):
- DWT is deadweight: maximum weight of cargo plus fuel, stores and other variable
  loads. It is not usable cargo capacity and is not the ship's own empty weight.
- Gross/net tonnage are measures based on enclosed volume, not cargo weight.
- Laycan is a loading-readiness/cancelling window. It is not laytime (time allowed
  for cargo operations). Exact contractual rights depend on the charter terms.
- A vessel's open area/window describes where/when it is expected to be available.
  Matching this to laycan does not establish sailing-time or arrival feasibility.
- Ballast means without cargo; laden means carrying cargo. Navigational status
  (e.g. anchored) and commercial status (e.g. fixed) describe different things.
- ETA is estimated arrival at a reported destination, not proof of readiness at
  an order's loading port. Date received/update date describe reports, not movements.
- Cargo quantity in the supplied schema is measured in metric tonnes.
- Dry-bulk freight involves unpackaged solid commodities carried in bulk. A cargo
  enquiry describes the cargo side; a tonnage report describes the vessel side.
