# Basemap source

Natural Earth 1:110m physical land polygons (public domain).
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson

Coordinates rounded to three decimal places. Antarctica omitted from this maritime overview. Polygon rings are retained; an equirectangular projection is shared by the basemap and regional markers. Data is served locally without a map service or runtime network dependency.
